Our History

Founded in 2016

Paws & Hope Animal Rescue began as a small group of volunteers fostering strays in their own homes. Ten years on, we operate a dedicated shelter and have rescued, rehabilitated and rehomed over 1,200 animals across our community.

Our Mission & Vision

Care, rehabilitate, rehome

Mission: To rescue vulnerable animals, provide veterinary care and rehabilitation, and find them safe, permanent homes.

Vision: A community where every companion animal is valued, cared for, and free from neglect.
